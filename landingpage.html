PRIVACY AND CONTROL

Safety without unnecessary surveillance.

We believe privacy is fundamental. You control who sees your information, and data is only accessible when it matters most for your safety.

1. CONTROLLED ACCESS

Users maintain absolute authority over which trusted guardians can access their data. Access is dynamically granted only for specific journeys or by invitation.

Review access controls architecture

2. MINIMAL DATA

The platform stores and references only the context signals necessary for the safety of the user. Journey data is handled securely after journeys conclude.

3. SECURE CONNECTION

Robust end-to-end authentication mechanisms protect the link between the user, their devices, Guardian Portal, and context evaluation server systems.
