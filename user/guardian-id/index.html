How Connection Works

Share this identifier with your guardian. Once they submit a link request from their console, you will receive an alert to review and approve their connection.

Privacy & Security

Your Guardian ID is not a password. It only starts the connection process. Sharing this ID does not grant access to your location or data until you explicitly approve the request.

1
Share Guardian ID

Copy and provide your code to your parent, spouse, caregiver, or trusted contact.

2
Guardian Requests Link

3
You Review & Approve

You accept the incoming request and configure individual permissions under your controls.
